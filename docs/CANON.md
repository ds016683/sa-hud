# Canon · what a thing is

The mission vocabulary of the Sovereign Architect HUD (David, 10/8/2026). Missions are nouns. The Loadout supplies capacity and verbs. A mission's type is how many slots it takes.

## Ad Hoc · 1 mile
What lands on you unplanned: a call, a thing that just came up. It rides the Ad Hoc slot, one at a time, and is dispatched, not planned. Never a Side Mission type; if it turns out to be real work, dispatch it into one.

## Bounty · 1 slot · 4 miles
A single discrete action with a clear finish, under an hour. An email, a call, a transcription, a read-and-reply, a quick decision, a thing to send. No steps, no artifact expected. Queue it in the morning or take it as it comes.

**Test:** can it be done in one sitting and be over? Bounty.

## Contract · 2 slots · 15 miles
A standard piece of work with a named deliverable and usually a counterparty. Review a proposal. Frame a concept paper. Research a subject. Draft a memo. A couple of hours, one or two sittings. It may carry an artifact or a file; it has no steps.

**Test:** is there a deliverable someone is waiting on, and will it take a couple of hours? Contract.

## Mythic · 3 slots · 50 miles, plus 10 per task (five at most)
Several steps that have to happen in order, with a story of its own, done within days. It takes the whole bag on the day it is worked. It is the only Side Mission type with the steps module: each step is a Mythic Task (10 miles, the first five). It carries steps, artifacts, files, and sometimes a session board.

**Test:** does closing it out take several ordered steps, but fit inside a week and your own hands? Mythic. A sixth step, or weeks of work with its own cabinet: that is a Main Mission.

## Main Mission · 100 miles on completion, plus 10 per Main Mission Task
A body of work with a destination that takes weeks or months. It owns artifacts, a file cabinet, shares, and session boards. Its tasks are milestones (MMTs): higher-level things that earn miles and must hold true downstream; the small steps live on session boards. One mission per outcome.

**Test:** will this still be open next month, and does it need its own cabinet? Main Mission.

## The slot economy
Three slots of planned work plus the Ad Hoc slot. A Bounty takes one, a Contract two, a Mythic all three. One clock runs at a time. Stamina (free hours before 6 PM) is the soft ceiling.

## Calibration
100 miles is a balanced, good, productive day. North of that is extra effort. South means a conscious decoupling or a distraction. No one thing is overweighted; nerf and buff as we go.
