# FUTURE DAVID — Morning Arrival
*~5 min guided meditation. Pause cues in brackets.*

---
Wherever you are, settle.

*[pause 2s]*

You don't have to close your eyes. Just let them soften. Look at something that doesn't move.

*[pause 2s]*

Breathe once, slow, all the way down.

*[pause 6s]*

You've been riding the rapids a long time. Longer than most people would survive.

*[pause 3s]*

And here's what's true today, that wasn't true a year ago: the steel cable is cut.

*[pause 3s]*

You're not tied to the rock anymore.

*[pause 2s]*

You're still moving fast. The water hasn't stilled. But you're not anchored.

*[pause 3s]*

You're traveling.

*[pause 5s]*

Let the river widen. The walls fall back. The white water softens.

*[pause 3s]*

Up ahead, somewhere you can't quite see yet, the river meets open water.

*[pause 3s]*

You're going there.

*[pause 5s]*

Now step forward in time. Twenty years.

*[pause 3s]*

The man you become is already there, waiting.

*[pause 4s]*

He's not a stranger. He's you, finished.

*[pause 4s]*

Look at him.

*[pause 5s]*

He's not in a hurry. He's not performing for anyone. There's no shoreline he's playing to.

*[pause 3s]*

He's sitting somewhere quiet — maybe a back porch, maybe a chair by a window —

*[pause 2s]*

and someone he loves just walked into the room.

*[pause 2s]*

His face moves before his words do.

*[pause 4s]*

He's home to them. He's home to himself.

*[pause 6s]*

You can feel what he's made of.

*[pause 3s]*

Kindness, the kind that doesn't ask to be noticed.

*[pause 3s]*

Patience, the kind that comes from knowing you have time.

*[pause 3s]*

Integrity that's gone so deep it isn't effort anymore — it's just how he is, alone and with everyone.

*[pause 3s]*

Loyalty that doesn't waver, because there's no calculation underneath it.

*[pause 4s]*

And the shadows — the ones you've spent your life hiding —

*[pause 2s]*

they're out in the open with him now. They're not weapons against him anymore. They're just part of the room.

*[pause 5s]*

He's the Rock. Not the one you were tied to.

*[pause 3s]*

The one you became.

*[pause 6s]*

He's looking at you now.

*[pause 3s]*

He has one thing to say to you about today.

*[pause 3s]*

Listen.

*[pause 9s]*

What is one thing today that the Rock would do?

*[pause 9s]*

Hold it. Don't argue with it. Just hold what came.

*[pause 6s]*

Now come back. The river is still moving. You're still traveling.

*[pause 3s]*

But you know where you're going. You've seen him.

*[pause 3s]*

He's real.

*[pause 4s]*

When you open your eyes, write down what he said.

*[pause 2s]*

That's your anchor today.

*[pause 6s]*
